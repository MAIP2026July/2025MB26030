# ModelTrace demonstration guide

1. Extract the ZIP and open `dist/index.html`.
2. Select Report with ambiguous backtesting and submit a new version. Show that the check starts automatically and the portal is the UI.
3. Open Backtesting. Inspect the requirement, candidate passage and High review priority. Explain that this is not confirmed gap severity.
4. Attempt Save decision before confirming the checklist. The app requires applicability confirmation.
5. Confirm the checklist. Choose Request clarification, enter a substantive reason and save. The review stays incomplete.
6. Choose Complete synthetic report and submit a new version. Show the earlier version is superseded but its clarification decision remains accessible. Complete six human decisions if demonstrating a completed evidence note; each needs a reason, and Evidence sufficient needs a cited paragraph.
7. Select Unreadable report demonstration and submit. Needs attention appears, no gap conclusion is issued, and manual review is required.
8. Export an evidence note. Open the JSON to show report/guidance IDs, proposed states, decisions, unresolved items and the explicit absence of model approval.

Use only synthetic reports. Demonstrated behavior is local browser workflow plus lexical rules. Do not claim bank integration, an LLM, production access controls, verified time savings or independent gap-detection accuracy.
